# mygame Domain Glossary

Canonical terms for the armored-train day/night mobile defense, CDDA-inspired urban expedition, and Gunsmith meta-progression loop.

## Language

### World & Time Cycle

**Expedition / Sector（隨機街區）**:
A procedurally generated urban map (built with the CDDA-inspired 5-stage pipeline) featuring a through-running railway corridor, nested civil-defense buildings, loot sockets, wandering hostiles, and strategic train parking terrain.
_Avoid_: Arena, arena-map, stage

**Day / Night Cycle（晝夜循環）**:
The continuous time progression (`Day` → `Dusk` → `Night` / `Blood Moon`) governing visibility, lighting, monster behavior, and whether the Armored Train is mobile along the tracks.
_Avoid_: Round timer, match clock

**Blood Moon（血月時刻）**:
An escalated crimson-sky night state triggered when the Blood Moon Gauge (`0–100`) reaches `100` via combined Time Accumulation, high-noise/high-reward Actions, and dynamic Random Events; spawns elite/boss threats that drop rare permanent progression cores and blueprints.
_Avoid_: Enrage mode, overtime

**Blood Moon Gauge（血月值）**:
The visible `0–100` threat meter driven by both predictable daily progression and player risk actions (plus random world modifiers), checked at Dusk to determine whether the upcoming night becomes a Blood Moon.
_Avoid_: Heat bar, wanted level

### Armored Train & Fortifications

**Armored Train（裝甲火車基地）**:
The player's fortified mobile base that drives forward/backward along the Sector's railway during Daytime and locks into powered siege-defense mode at Night.
_Avoid_: Hub, camp, vehicle

**Fortification / Field Defense（臨時陣地工事）**:
Tactical ground structures (sandbags, barbed barricades, proximity mines, fixed sentry turrets, spotlights) built around the train's chosen parking position to survive nightly sieges; left behind when departing the Sector.
_Avoid_: Tower, building

**Anchor Facility（大型留守設施）**:
A fixed high-value industrial/energy structure in a Sector (oil extractor, military forge, decryption tower) that requires the Armored Train to dock nearby for power/linkup and produces escalating high-tier rewards over consecutive days stayed.
_Avoid_: Quest node, generator prop

**Train Stash（列車暫存倉）**:
The protected cargo space aboard the Armored Train where gathered Resources deposited mid-expedition are safeguarded aboard the train.
_Avoid_: Inventory, bank, bag

### Threats & Progression

**Wandering Infected（日間遊蕩怪）**:
Scattered infected patrolling urban streets and building interiors during Daytime; react to sightlines and gunfire noise rather than converging across the map.
_Avoid_: Trash mobs

**Night Siege / Horde Wave（夜間圍攻屍潮）**:
Organized infected waves spawned at Night that converge on the Armored Train's current parked position and surrounding Fortifications, scaling in strength with days spent in the same Sector.
_Avoid_: Mob pack, ambush

**Gunsmith（槍匠系統）**:
The primary permanent meta-progression workshop allowing firearm unlocks, base weapon upgrades, and tactical attachments (muzzles/suppressors, magazines, underbarrel lights) that interact directly with stealth, Blood Moon buildup, and night visibility.
_Avoid_: Shop, crafting bench

**Crew Member（NPC 乘員）**:
A non-player specialist rescued or recruited across expeditions and assigned to a station aboard the Armored Train to provide passive or active support.
_Avoid_: Companion, squadmate, follower, ally

**Locomotive（裝甲火車頭）**:
The lead engine unit of the Armored Train whose upgrades determine maximum towed Carriage count, Power Budget, base Hull HP, and reachable Sector tiers.
_Avoid_: Head car, front truck

**Carriage（掛接車廂）**:
An individual modular train car coupled behind the Locomotive that can be expanded, reordered, and refitted into specialized functional types (Turret, Cargo, Medbay, Armory, Recon, Quarters, Generator).
_Avoid_: Room, cabin, compartment, wagon

**Carriage Refit（車廂改造）**:
Converting or upgrading a coupled Carriage into a specific functional role, analogous to cabin refitting in *Uncharted Waters*.
_Avoid_: Respec, rebuild

**Crew Station（車廂崗位槽）**:
An assignment slot inside the Locomotive or a refitted Carriage where a Crew Member can be stationed to activate or amplify bonuses based on role affinity.
_Avoid_: Seat, socket, party slot

**Resource（戰利品資源）**:
Salvageable materials (`scrap`, `parts`, `fuel`, `supplies`) and rare blueprints/cores collected during expeditions to build Fortifications, repair the train, and fund Gunsmith & Train Upgrades.
_Avoid_: Currency, gold, drop
