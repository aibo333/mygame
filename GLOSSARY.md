# mygame Domain Glossary

Canonical terms for the armored-train urban extraction and horde-defense game loop.

## Language

### Run & World

**Run（單局出擊）**:
A single timed expedition in which the Armored Train docks at a newly generated Sector and the player attempts to gather Resources, finish the Mission Objective, and Extract.
_Avoid_: Match, level, stage

**Sector（隨機街區）**:
A procedurally generated urban map composed of modular city blocks, streets, loot points, and a railway docking edge created for one Run.
_Avoid_: Arena, map, zone

**Armored Train（裝甲火車基地）**:
The player's fortified mobile base docked at the Sector's railway edge during a Run, serving as deployment point, defense anchor, Stash drop-off, and Extraction vehicle.
_Avoid_: Hub, camp, vehicle

**Train Stash（列車暫存倉）**:
The protected cargo space aboard the Armored Train where gathered Resources deposited mid-Run are safeguarded against losing carried backpack loot.
_Avoid_: Inventory, bank, bag

### Threats & Objectives

**Mission Objective（單局任務）**:
The primary task assigned for a Run that must be completed before normal Extraction departure can be triggered.
_Avoid_: Quest, contract

**Horde Wave（屍潮波次）**:
An escalating surge of hostile infected spawned into the Sector as the Run timer advances or when Extraction ignition draws attention.
_Avoid_: Mob pack, ambush

**Extraction（撤離）**:
The final holdout and departure sequence aboard the Armored Train that ends a Run in victory and secures collected Resources and rescued Crew Members.
_Avoid_: Escape, evacuation, finish

### Meta-Progression

**Crew Member（NPC 乘員）**:
A non-player specialist rescued or recruited across Runs and assigned to a station aboard the Armored Train to provide passive or active support.
_Avoid_: Companion, squadmate, follower, ally

**Train Upgrade（火車強化）**:
A permanent structural, defensive, engine, armory, or cargo enhancement installed on the Armored Train between Runs using extracted Resources.
_Avoid_: Buff, perk, level-up

**Resource（戰利品資源）**:
Salvageable materials (such as scrap, parts, fuel, and supplies) collected during a Run to sustain survival and fund Train Upgrades between Runs.
_Avoid_: Currency, gold, drop
